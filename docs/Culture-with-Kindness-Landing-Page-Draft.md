# Proposed landing-page copy

## 1. Opening proposition

**Culture with Kindness**

**A kinder culture. A stronger team.**

Trauma-informed training, resources and bespoke practice support.

For cultural organisations working across exhibitions, music, theatre, performance, public programmes and participatory arts. Strengthen care and consistency across staff, freelancers and volunteers, including programming, participation, visitor experience, production and leadership teams.

We're inviting arts and cultural organisations to join the Culture with Kindness pilot programme.

**Primary CTA: Discuss your organisation’s needs**

## 2. Why organisations choose the programme

Cultural organisations bring together many roles, settings and ways of working. Build shared approaches to care, choice and consent, with clear boundaries and shared responsibility—so care does not rest on a few people.

In its consultation with more than 50 cultural organisations, Clean Break heard a shared challenge: building a consistent understanding of trauma-informed practice across teams. It also heard that people championing this work could feel unsupported when responsibility for care was not shared. Culture with Kindness responds with practical training, resources and space for reflection. These are reported consultation findings, not proof of programme impact.

## 3. What the offer includes

**Practical training:** trauma-informed sessions for staff, freelancers and volunteers, suited to different roles and levels of experience.

**Online resources:** practical tools for teams working directly with audiences, participants and artists, so learning can be revisited in everyday work.

**Included support:** check-ins, attendance reports and updates on upcoming opportunities, to help your organisation make full use of the programme.

Bespoke practice support can be discussed for your context; its scope is agreed with you. Its inclusion or price is not assumed.

## 4. Four workshops

### An Introduction to Trauma-Informed Practice

An accessible introduction to the core principles of trauma-informed practice and how these can be applied in creative, participatory and organisational settings.

### An Introduction to Trauma-Informed Facilitation

For early career facilitators, or facilitators new to trauma-informed practice. This practical session explores safety, boundaries, responding with care and building equity and empowerment.

### Responding to Challenging Situations

For public-facing staff. This session explores how distress may affect behaviour and how a trauma-informed approach can support de-escalation with care and clear boundaries.

### Caring for Stories

For anyone making or researching work from lived experience. Co-designed with women artists with lived experience of the criminal justice system, this session centres care, consent and choice in how stories are shared.

## 5. How it works for your organisation

1. **Discuss your needs:** talk with Clean Break about your organisation, teams and priorities.
2. **Agree a suitable package:** confirm the training and resources that are right for your context.
3. **Share access information:** Clean Break provides the information participants need to select and book relevant training.
4. **Keep connected:** receive check-ins, attendance reports and updates on upcoming opportunities.

## 6. The participant experience

People choose sessions relevant to their roles and practice. They book and attend training at Clean Break, develop practical skills and connect with peers from across the cultural and social sectors. Detailed booking information follows organisational agreement.

## 7. Why Clean Break

Clean Break is a theatre company with a 45-year track record of holding creative spaces with safety and care at their heart. Its decades of creative work with women who are criminalised inform its approach to supporting confident, consistent trauma-informed practice.

Depending on the agreed scope, work is led by experienced Clean Break trauma-informed leads and trainers, drawing on creative practice and organisational learning. The supplied presentation profiles Anna Herrmann, Artistic Director and Joint CEO, and Jacqueline Stewart, Head of Participation and Deputy CEO. The website uses concise summaries of their source biographies rather than adding claims.

## 8. Bespoke practice support

**Practice review:** a review of current practice with an experienced trauma-informed practitioner, offering practical recommendations relevant to your organisation.

**Creative project support:** bespoke support for upcoming exhibitions, performances and events, including lived-experience feedback.

**Stakeholder presentations:** presentations on embedding trauma-informed practice through shared responsibility and organisational learning.

Discuss the scope that would be useful for your organisation. No inclusion or fee is implied before agreement.

## 9. Practical information and enquiry

Half-day, in-person sessions at Clean Break's premises in Kentish Town, grounded in its approach to care and participation. Participants learn alongside people from across the cultural and social sectors.

Tell us about your organisation, the people you’d like to involve and the priorities you’re working with. We’ll discuss whether Culture with Kindness is a good fit and explain the programme, arrangements and next steps.

**Discuss your organisation’s needs**

An enquiry doesn’t commit you to joining.
